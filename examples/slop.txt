Manning an event booth is actually such a treat when you're a marketer talking to marketers about one of the most contentious topics in marketing -- the website.

Marketers have a lot of feelings about their website, as they should!

- it's the crux of every campaign

- changes are gated behind resources they don't control 

- teams are measured on things like on-page conversion, campaign CTR, and new leads. they have all the responsibility without the power to make changes 

- and they've got ideas! so many ideas!

I remember the excitement of just knowing a new experiment or campaign would work, but also knowing there was no way I'd be able to prioritize it enough for it to see the light of day.

Having used Ploy every day for the past 3 months, I had a blast sharing the gospel with other eager marketers.
